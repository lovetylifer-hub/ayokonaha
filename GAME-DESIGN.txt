DEJA BREW: THE THINGS WE LEAVE UNSAID
Chapter 01: The Last Table

VISION
An original first-person, cinematic narrative adventure about friendship, change, memory, and the things people leave unsaid. Mood: rainy evenings, warm café lights, quiet environmental storytelling, restrained dialogue, and choices that affect relationships.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE
- First-person WASD movement and mouse-look.
- Explore a detailed café interior during a rainy evening.
- Discover a letter on the protagonist's usual table.
- Speak to Rei and choose a response style.
- Uncover the first layer of why Tyli left.
- Chapter-end beat teases future story branches.

PROTOTYPE CONTROLS
WASD: move
Mouse: look around (click the scene to enable pointer lock on desktop)
E: interact
Enter: continue dialogue
Escape: close dialogue
On touch devices, drag to look; keyboard controls require a physical keyboard. A mobile on-screen joystick is a planned improvement.

REALISTIC IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
Vertical slice 1 (current): single café scene, first-person camera, mood lighting, rain particles, letter discovery, branching dialogue, chapter ending.
Vertical slice 2: replace blockout props with authored café assets, add footsteps/rain/room tone, improve collision, add touch joystick and interaction reticle, add accessible subtitle controls.
Vertical slice 3: create character models and staged conversation scenes, add relationship flags and save/load, add 2-3 more explorable locations.
Release-quality target: authored 3D assets and animation, optimized lighting, tested controls on target browsers/devices, narrative QA, audio mix, and persistent save system.

TECH NOTES
Three.js is imported from jsDelivr as an ES module. Use a local server for testing (python -m http.server 8000), then open http://localhost:8000. GitHub Pages can host it; internet is required for CDN assets.
This is a playable proof-of-concept, not a finished commercial game. Some environment props are procedural blockout geometry. More advanced collision, mobile joystick, character animation, sound, and persistent save are not included yet.
